# Move your OneDrive folder to your big drive

OneDrive keeps a copy of all your cloud files on your main drive. Moving that local copy to your big drive frees up space without affecting anything in the cloud — your files stay safe in OneDrive online.

## Steps

1. Find the **OneDrive** icon in your taskbar. It looks like a small white or blue cloud, usually in the bottom-right corner of your screen. If you don't see it, click the little up arrow (^) to show hidden icons.

2. Click the cloud icon once. A small panel opens.

3. Click the **gear icon** (⚙) in the top-right of that panel, then click **Settings**.

4. In the Settings window, click the **Account** tab.

5. Click **Unlink this PC**. Don't worry — this just disconnects the sync temporarily. Your files stay in the cloud.

6. After unlinking, OneDrive will show a sign-in screen. **Don't sign in yet.**

7. Open **File Explorer** and move the existing OneDrive folder to your big drive. (Right-click → Cut, navigate to your D: drive, right-click → Paste.)

8. Now go back to OneDrive and sign in with your Microsoft account.

9. When it asks where to put your OneDrive folder, click **Change location** and point it to the folder you just moved on your big drive.

10. Click **Next** and let it sync. This may take a few minutes.

## You're done

OneDrive now lives on your big drive. Everything syncs the same as before — you just have more room on your main drive.
