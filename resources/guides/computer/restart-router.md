# Restart your router to fix the internet

When the internet is slow or not working, restarting your router fixes it about 80% of the time. It's the first thing to try.

## What you need

Your router — the box that connects to the internet. It usually has blinking lights on it and is plugged into the wall and into a cable that comes from outside your house. Sometimes the router and the modem are two separate boxes; restart both.

## Steps

1. Find the router (and modem, if separate).

2. Unplug the power cord from the back. If there's a power button, you can press that instead.

3. Wait **30 full seconds**. This is important — it needs time to fully reset.

4. Plug it back in (or press the power button again).

5. Wait about **2 minutes** for all the lights to settle down and turn solid (not flashing).

6. Try the internet again.

## The lights look normal but internet still doesn't work?

That usually means the problem is with your internet provider, not your equipment. You can:
- Call your internet provider and report an outage
- Check if neighbors are having the same problem
- Try again in an hour — outages usually fix themselves

## You're done

If the internet came back, you're all set!
