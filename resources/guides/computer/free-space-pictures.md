# Move your Pictures folder to your big drive

Your Pictures folder is on your main drive (the C: drive), which is almost full. Moving it to your big drive frees up a lot of space — and nothing changes about how you open photos. Windows will find them in exactly the same place.

## Before you start

Make sure your big drive is plugged in and showing up. It usually has a name like "D:" or "Photos Drive."

## Steps

1. **Open File Explorer** — it looks like a yellow folder. You can find it on your taskbar at the bottom of the screen.

2. **Find your Pictures folder** on the left side. Right-click on it (press the right mouse button once).

3. In the menu that pops up, click **Properties**.

4. Click the tab at the top that says **Location**.

5. Click the button that says **Move…**

6. A window opens. Click on your big drive on the left side (look for D: or whatever your big drive is called).

7. Click **New Folder** at the bottom, and name it **Pictures**.

8. Click **Select Folder**, then click **OK**.

9. Windows will ask: *"Do you want to move all the files from the old location to the new location?"* Click **Yes**.

10. Wait for it to finish — this may take a few minutes if you have a lot of photos.

## You're done!

Your photos are now on your big drive. Your Pictures folder in the left sidebar still works the same way — click it and all your photos will be right there.
