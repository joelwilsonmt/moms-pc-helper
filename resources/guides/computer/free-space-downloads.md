# Clean up your Downloads folder

Your Downloads folder collects everything you've ever downloaded — software installers, PDFs, photos from email, forms you filled out years ago. Most of it you don't need anymore.

## Steps

1. **Open File Explorer** (the yellow folder on your taskbar).

2. Click **Downloads** on the left side.

3. At the top of the window, click **View**, then choose **Details**. This shows you the date each file was downloaded.

4. Click the **Date modified** column header to sort by date — oldest files go to the bottom.

5. Scroll down to the older files. Anything from more than a year ago that you don't recognize is almost certainly safe to delete.

6. To delete a file: click it once to select it, then press the **Delete** key on your keyboard. It goes to the Recycle Bin first — it's not gone yet.

7. If you want to delete several files at once: click the first one, hold the **Shift** key, and click the last one. Then press **Delete**.

8. When you're done, right-click the **Recycle Bin** on your desktop and choose **Empty Recycle Bin** to actually free the space.

## What's safe to delete

- Files ending in `.exe` or `.msi` — these are installers for programs you've already installed (or never installed)
- Files ending in `.zip` — usually things you already unzipped
- Old PDFs, especially forms and receipts from years ago

## What to keep

- Anything you're not sure about — leave it. Disk space is cheap compared to the hassle of re-finding something important.
